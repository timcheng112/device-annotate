Color for identity; symbols and borders for results

My recommendation for this alternative: give each annotation an identity color, keep its number, and use explicit ✓ PASS / × FAIL cues. Solid/dashed borders reinforce the result. The color choice itself would not indicate success or failure.

Colors help separate overlapping targets. They do not prevent one identical outline from covering another, and still need white halos against matching app backgrounds.
